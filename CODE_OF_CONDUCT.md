# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the `runtime-evidence` community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, ability, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Welcome questions and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security reporting channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, keep details confidential where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a reviewer.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
