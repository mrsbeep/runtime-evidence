# Governance

`runtime-evidence` uses a maintainer-led, consensus-seeking governance model during its early development.

## Roles

- **Contributors** participate in issues, discussions, documentation, code, testing, or review.
- **Maintainers** review contributions, manage releases, enforce community policies, and protect the project's security and product principles.

Maintainers are expected to act in the project's interest, disclose conflicts, and apply policies consistently. New maintainers may be invited based on sustained, high-quality participation and sound judgment, with agreement from the existing maintainers.

## Decisions

Routine decisions are made through issues and pull requests. Maintainers seek consensus for architectural, schema, security, roadmap, and governance changes. When consensus is not possible, maintainers document the decision and its rationale publicly unless security or privacy requires confidentiality.

## Project commitments

- Correctness and security fixes remain available in the Apache-2.0 community project.
- Anonymous usage telemetry remains off by default.
- Design discussions and roadmap planning are public by default.
- Official, experimental, and community-supported components are clearly labeled.

This governance model will evolve as the contributor community grows. Material changes require public discussion.
